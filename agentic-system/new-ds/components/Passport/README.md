# Passport
The passport booklet and the tap-to-onboard scene: passport, three fading gold signal arcs, phone with a gold check.

- Passport body is `night`; the globe, "PASSPORT" caps (letter-spacing 0.28em) and chip are `gold`. Phone outline is `ink` at 4px with a `gold` disc and `on-gold` check.
- The three arcs run `gold`, `gold-mid`, `gold-soft` from the source outward and mean "reading from the chip"; always draw them in that order.
- Drawn to the deck's style from the slides, not copied from a file; no real country's passport design or crest is depicted. Caption the scene with "Onboard once through tapping."
