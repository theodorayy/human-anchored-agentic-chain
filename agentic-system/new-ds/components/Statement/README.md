# Statement
The deck's core layout: a centred one-idea statement in light weight where the payoff phrase flips to bold and `gold`, under a small `eyebrow` lead-in.

- Structure: `eyebrow` in `ink-subtle` (the first words bold), then `display-xl` or `display-lg` in 300, then optional `lede` in `ink-muted`. Only one gold phrase per slide, at 700.
- Fill the slide with white `surface`; use the dark theme (`data-theme="dark"`, `surface` becomes `#1c1c1f`, gold becomes pale yellow) once per story for the pivot ("Too heavy, she pays. Too light, you pay.").
- Left-aligned variant for persona slides: `display-lg`, with a three-card `StepCard` row beneath. Footnotes use `footnote`, bottom-left.
- Copy is in British English, second person or "she", short sentences; fragments may end in full stops.
- Put `HillMark`'s progress dot bottom-right on every light slide except the title and introduction slides.
